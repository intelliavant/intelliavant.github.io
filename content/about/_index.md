Intelliavant Technologies, Ltd. is a software house based in Canada, founded in 2018.
We design and build mobile apps, websites and web applications for businesses and
individuals, and we also publish mobile apps of our own.

## What we do

- **Mobile app development:** native and cross-platform apps that are fast, reliable
  and easy to use.
- **Website building:** clean, responsive websites that put small businesses and
  individuals on the map.
- **Web app development:** custom online systems built around how your business and
  your users actually work.

## How we work

We believe good software starts with listening. Before writing a line of code, we
take the time to understand your goals, your users and your constraints. From there
we work in small, visible steps, with regular demos and honest estimates, so you
always know where your project stands.

We care about the parts of software you don't see: clean, tested and maintainable
code that keeps working long after launch. And when the project is delivered, we
stay around to support it and help it grow.

## What we value

- **Simplicity:** the best solution is often the simplest one that works.
- **Quality:** we build things to last, not just to ship.
- **Privacy:** we collect only what we need and never sell personal information.
  See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
- **Partnership:** your success is how we measure ours.
