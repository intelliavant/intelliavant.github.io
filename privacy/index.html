Privacy Policy

Intelliavant Technologies, Ltd.
Effective date: November 24, 2026

Intelliavant Technologies, Ltd. (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, intelliavant.com, and to the mobile apps we publish (see section 9). It is written to meet Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other privacy laws that apply to us.

1. Who we are

Intelliavant Technologies, Ltd. is a company based in Canada. Our privacy contact is listed in section 12.

2. Our principles

  • We collect only the personal information we need for a clear purpose.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use personal information for advertising.
  • We keep it only as long as needed, and protect it while we have it.

3. Information we collect through our website

  • Information you give us. When you contact us by email, we receive what you send, such as your name, email address, company and message.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of the visit. We use it to keep the site running and secure.
  • Cookies and analytics. Our website uses only cookies that are needed for it to work. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • reply to your questions and requests;
  • provide, maintain and secure our website, products and services;
  • meet our legal, accounting and contractual obligations.

We will ask for your consent before using personal information for any other purpose.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers who work for us (for example, website hosting and email), who may use it only to provide those services to us;
  • when required by law, or to protect our rights, our users or the public;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under this policy.

6. Where information is stored

We and our service providers may store and process information in Canada and in other countries, such as the United States. Information stored in another country is subject to that country’s laws, and may be accessed by its courts and authorities.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law, and then delete or anonymize it.

8. How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information against loss, theft and unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Our mobile apps

Unless stated otherwise for a specific app, our apps do not collect, transmit or share any personal information:

  • Your data stays on your device. Content you create in our apps and your app settings are stored only on your device. Our apps have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and do not send this information to us or to anyone else.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling an app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, removes all of its data.
  • Device backup. If your device’s backup feature is turned on, your operating system may include app data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account) so it can be restored on a new device. That backup is handled by the backup provider; we have no access to it.
  • Permissions. Our apps request device permissions (such as notifications, alarms or vibration) only to provide their features, and not to collect information about you.

Apps distributed through Google Play are also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may give us crash and performance statistics about our apps; these do not identify you.

10. Children

Our website and apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Your rights

You may ask to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to its use (which may limit what we can do for you). Contact us as described in section 12; we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change significantly affects how we handle your personal information, we will make it clear on this page.
